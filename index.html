The Duel Awaits

A fully custom Yu-Gi-Oh!-style card game — original monsters, spells and traps, seven playable decks, and an Extra Deck full of Fusion, Synchro and Xyz summons. Play against an AI with four difficulty tiers, build your own deck, or create brand new cards from scratch.

PLAY

Duel Engine

Pick a premade deck, a saved custom deck, or generate one on the fly, then duel a full AI opponent — tribute summons, trap timing, the Graveyard, and Extra Deck plays all included.

CREATE

Card Creator

Design your own monsters, spells and traps. Anything you make here shows up as a real, playable card in the Duel Engine's Deck Maker.

What's inside

  • 7 premade decks — Dragon, Spellcaster, Warrior, Machine, Zombie, Fairy and Fiend archetypes
  • Deck Maker — build and edit custom decks from the entire card pool, with filters and live Extra Deck validation
  • Deck Generator — auto-build a deck around Aggro, Trickery, Support or Burn
  • 4 difficulty tiers — including a Tutorial mode with a guided walkthrough
  • Graveyard & revival effects — Monster Reborn, mill strategies, and Graveyard-scaling cards
  • Duel Analyzer — a full stat breakdown after every match, yours to view or skip
  • Secret cards — a few are hidden until you go looking for them
  • Version history — every card is tagged with the release it first appeared in
